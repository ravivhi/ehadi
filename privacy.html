אחדי - אלופי המספרים – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

אחדי - אלופי המספרים ("the app", also called Mathoria) is a free math learning game for children in grades 1–6, made by Raviv Hilleli. We do not collect personal information.

What the app does with your data

  • The app works offline. No personal data is sent from your device to us or to any third party.
  • The child's name, grade, avatar, progress and settings are saved only in your device's local storage and can be removed by clearing the app's data.
  • Questions may be read aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech, which runs on the device. If the device has no voice for the app's language (for example no Hebrew voice), the text of the sentence being read (such as a math question) is sent to our speech service, which uses Google Cloud Text-to-Speech to turn it into audio. Only that sentence and its language are sent: never the child's name or any other personal information. The audio is saved on the device so the same sentence is not requested again.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Children

The app is designed for children. The name a parent types stays on the device and is never sent anywhere. Parents can contact us with any question.
